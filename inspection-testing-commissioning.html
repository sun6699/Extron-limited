Inspection, Testing & Commissioning Support

Project lifecycle support from engineering through maintenance and integrity management

Our technical support extends across the project lifecycle: Engineering → Procurement → Inspection → Installation → Testing → Commissioning → Maintenance → Integrity Management. Services may include factory acceptance testing (FAT), site acceptance testing (SAT), equipment inspection, instrument calibration, loop checking, pressure testing, functional testing, pre-commissioning, commissioning support, equipment verification and technical documentation.

We assist clients to ensure installed systems meet design intent, regulatory standards and safe operational requirements before handover and operational deployment.
