About Extron Nigeria Limited

Professional Engineering Support & Project Management Consultancy

Extron Nigeria Limited is a multi-disciplinary company established in 2010. We engage in engineering design, project management consultancy, procurement, construction, telecommunication and supply of all forms of equipment related to oil, manufacturing and other sectors of the economy.

We are committed to meeting international standards of corporate organization by protecting the health and safety of our employees, safeguarding the environment, and creating a long-lasting positive impact on the communities in which we do business.

With a strong leadership team and technical partnerships with global organizations, we implement a focused strategy that positions Extron Nigeria Limited with long-term support and outstanding team performance.

Our Vision

To render quality services not just to satisfy our clients but to be the best one-stop destination for engineering, installation, commissioning, start-up and maintenance services of Electrical, Instrumentation and control systems for oil and gas, Manufacturing, Telecommunication and government parastatals.

Our Mission

Extron Nigeria Limited will conduct its business activities in a manner that is consistent with the best international practice, with a strong commitment to safety and respect for the environment. We provide timely and responsive solutions ensuring value for money to all our customers.
