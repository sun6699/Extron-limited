About Extron Nigeria Limited

Professional Engineering Support & Project Management Consultancy

Who We Are

Extron Nigeria Limited is a multi-disciplinary company established in 2010, registered under the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) with registration number RC 864056. We specialize in providing comprehensive engineering design, project management consultancy, procurement, construction, and infrastructure development solutions tailored to the oil and gas industry.

Our team comprises experienced professionals with extensive backgrounds in engineering, project delivery, and technical support. We pride ourselves on delivering innovative solutions that drive operational excellence and value creation for our clients.
