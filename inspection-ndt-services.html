Oilfield Inspection & NDT Services

Professional Inspection and Non-Destructive Testing for Drilling and Oilfield Equipment

Extron Nigeria Limited provides comprehensive inspection and condition-assessment services for drilling, production, and oilfield equipment. Our certified inspection personnel and NDT technicians utilize industry-standard procedures and equipment to ensure equipment integrity, compliance, and operational reliability.

Our inspection services are supported by appropriate inspection procedures, rigorous quality documentation, complete traceability, and comprehensive reporting to meet operator standards and regulatory requirements.
