Technical Procurement & Oilfield Equipment Supply

Quality-driven sourcing of drilling, instrumentation and industrial products

We provide technical procurement and supply support for oilfield, drilling, instrumentation, mechanical and electrical requirements. Our work is based on technical specifications, approved manufacturers, quality requirements and client standards, with appropriate documentation and traceability.

From drilling tools and wellhead components to pressure transmitters, industrial valves, pumps and maintenance consumables, we help clients source quality equipment that meets project requirements and compliance expectations.

Procurement Categories

Drilling & Well Equipment

  • Drilling tools and drill-string components
  • BHA components, stabilizers and subs
  • Drill collars and heavyweight drill pipe
  • Wellhead components and completion accessories
  • Well intervention and workover equipment
  • Pressure-control equipment

Instrumentation & Control

  • Pressure transmitters and gauges
  • Temperature and flow measurement instruments
  • Level instruments and meters
  • Control valves and shutdown valves
  • Gas detectors and fire & gas equipment
  • PLC, RTU and SCADA components

Mechanical & Industrial Equipment

  • Industrial valves and pump systems
  • Pipes, fittings and flanges
  • Gaskets, bolts, seals and O-rings
  • Mechanical spares and components
  • Maintenance consumables
  • Bearing and seal assemblies

Quality & Compliance

  • Vendor coordination and support
